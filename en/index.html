Who we are

3-Star Hotel in Edea

Located in Edea, on the banks of the Sanaga river, our hostellerie enjoys a strategic location: about 60 km from Douala, the economic capital, 187 km from Yaoundé, the political capital, and 115 km from Kribi, a popular seaside destination. A secure and peaceful setting, designed to welcome business travelers and tourists alike.

The establishment is a Cameroonian company, a subsidiary of the Alucam Group, specializing in hospitality and food service.
